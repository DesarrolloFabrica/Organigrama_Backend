## Granularity answers (A–F)

### A. Full Stack

Not a V1 specialty. “Full Stack” as job title ≠ taxonomy. Legitimate profile shape is **co-observation** of `FRONTEND_ENGINEERING` + `BACKEND_ENGINEERING`. Avoids forced third identity and double-counting traps.

### B. Platform / DevOps

Belongs under **SOFTWARE_ENGINEERING** when practice enables **delivery of software being built** (CI/CD, packaging, app platform).  
**IT_SYSTEMS** when practice is operate/support enterprise infra, servers, helpdesk **without** software construction.

### C. Software Architecture

**Capability avanzada** inside Backend / Platform / (sometimes) Frontend — **DEFER** as specialty.

### D. QA / Test Automation

**Skill/capability** feeding dimension `SE_ENG_QUALITY` in V1. Dedicated QA specialty **DEFER**.

### E. Data Engineering

Primarily **DATA_ANALYTICS** / future Data field. Not SE specialty in V1.

### F. AI Software Engineering

Identity not stable enough. Keep **dimension** `SE_APPLIED_AI` + future skills. **DEFER** specialty.
